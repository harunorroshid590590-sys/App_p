About Footmad IPTV Player

Footmad IPTV Player is a native media player for playlists and stream addresses supplied by the user. The app does not include, sell, host or promote channels, movies, subscriptions or copyrighted streams.

Our goal is to provide clean local playlist management and reliable playback for content users are legally authorised to access.
